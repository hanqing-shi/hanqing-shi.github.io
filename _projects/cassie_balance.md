We used feedback control to determine the force and momentum that are required to balance. Then proposed a optimization problem to distribute contact forces. Finally, we calculated the torque using Cassie's equation of motion assuming the quasi-static motion. The video shows that the robot can resist constant forces coming from x and y direction.
